✨ Features

Everything your cafe needs

Built for real cafes. No technical knowledge required. Set up in under two minutes.

Live push notifications

Every stamp sends a banner to the customer's lock screen — no action needed from staff or customer. They know the moment it happens.

Fully branded per cafe

Upload your logo, pick your accent colour, choose your stamp icon. The card your customers carry looks like it was made just for you.

Lives in Apple Wallet

Always in their pocket. No app, no login, no friction.

Print-ready QR poster

Download a branded QR code for your counter. Customers scan to sign up.

Multi-cafe ready

Run any number of locations. Each gets its own card and PIN.

🗂️ How it works

Three steps to launch

1

Set up your cafe

Name, logo, accent colour, staff PIN. Done in two minutes at /setup. You get a signup link and staff terminal link immediately.

freecoffee.app/setup
2

Share your signup link

Print the QR code for your counter, or share the link on Instagram. Customers tap once to get their branded card saved to their phone.

freecoffee.app/signup?cafe=lucky-cafe
3

Stamp and redeem

Staff open the terminal on any phone. Scan the customer's Wallet QR, enter the PIN, tap stamp. The card updates live and a notification fires.
